## Culture Quest audio and learning limits

Culture Quest and proverb sentences use recorded standard comparisons where an exact matching native example is available; other lines use Microsoft Nanami standard Japanese synthesis via the existing project's speech pipeline. Every line identifies the audio type. Synthesized readings have **not** received an independent native-speaker accent audit. They do not certify exact pitch accent.

The new regional explorer does not feed regional sentences into a Tokyo voice or claim synthesis is an authentic regional accent. Native sentence-level playback is implemented for the 32 Kansai comparison units (some units contain short exchanges). Other regions have longer source recordings or external listening links. Fully aligned individual-sentence regional playback remains unfinished.

## Regional sentence cards added in this update

Sixteen cards now cover eight regions beyond Kansai. Twelve historical utterances are selected directly from the NINJAL volume 9 transcripts for Aomori, Gunma, Fukui, Shimane, Kochi, and Nagasaki. Exact source line IDs and localities are retained. Display-only footnote numbers are removed; original transcription and Japanese translation remain available on each card. These are source quotations under CC BY 4.0, not newly invented dialect sentences or claims about every current speaker.

Two Hokkaido scenarios were newly authored using the documented expressions なまら and したっけね. Reference: [Hokkaido tourism Easy Japanese handbook](https://www.visit-hokkaido.jp/lsc/upfile/document/0000/0028/28_1_file.pdf). These written examples have not received an independent local-speaker audit or verified regional recording. No handbook illustration or recording was copied.

Two short Shuri Okinawan utterances are quoted from NINJAL’s original Momotaro page, attributed to speaker 真栄平房敬, with a source link on each card. Source punctuation is normalized. The original external recording is linked, not copied. Shuri Okinawan is explicitly identified as a Ryukyuan language.

All sixteen standard Japanese meaning bridges have synthesized audio. This is labeled as **standard Japanese**, not as regional pronunciation. Regional per-sentence clips remain unavailable for these new cards; historic cards link the longer native archive, and Shuri cards link the original story. The UI states the difference next to the audio controls.

For readability, the historical cards’ standard Japanese meaning bridges are newly phrased equivalents with natural punctuation; they are not verbatim quotations of the archival translation. The unaltered source translation remains under “Original source transcription.” Local phonetic utterances and source IDs are preserved.

The quotations are brief language-study excerpts; rights in manga/anime remain with their creators and rights holders. No panels, scene footage, actor recordings, or character voice imitations are bundled. Each quotation card links to an official publisher, production, distributor, or source text.
