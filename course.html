A course you can make your own.

550 lessons in 110 units: 50 Foundation, and 100 each at N5, N4, N3, N2 and N1. Each unit has separate language, listening, reading, writing and speaking lessons.

These bands describe content difficulty. They are not an official JLPT syllabus or a guarantee of proficiency. Speaking and writing are additional skills, not JLPT test sections.

Audio is synthesized standard Japanese. This draft curriculum has not received independent linguistic validation. Quizzes are checked; writing and speaking use clear self-review rubrics, not automatic language grades.

Drafts and progress stay in this browser. Voice recordings are never uploaded and last only while the page stays open unless you download them. Optional microphone access is requested only when you choose Record.
